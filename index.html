NoteNirman — an offline-first notes app for Windows, Android and the web

NoteNirman is a notes app built for students. Notes save to your device first and sync when you're back online, so a lost connection never costs you your work. It has a block-style editor with tables, equations, flashcards, templates and notes linked with [[wiki links]], seasonal themes tied to Indian festivals, and an optional Gemini AI assistant. It's free to use, and available for Windows, Android and the web.

Frequently asked questions

What is Notenirman?
An offline-first notes app for Windows, Android and the web, with a block-style editor, seasonal Indian-festival themes, and an optional Gemini AI assistant.
Does Notenirman work offline?
Yes. Every note is saved to your device first and syncs automatically once you're back online.
Can I add tables and equations to my notes?
Yes, from the editor's slash (/) menu.
Does Notenirman have flashcards?
Yes. Write "Question:: Answer" in a note and it becomes a spaced-repetition flashcard.
Is Notenirman free to use?
Yes.
